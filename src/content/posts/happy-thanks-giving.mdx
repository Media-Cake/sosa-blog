Being Thanks Giving and us being British we were going to post something silly today and as funny as that would have been I wanted to write something a bit more heart felt.

It’s almost 4 years since [SoSa ](https://chatplayshare.com)was launched and with the support of our community we’re getting better every year.

Every week we’re doing a [game night](https://www.youtube.com/c/ChatPlayShare), every day we’re seeing 200-300 awesome and wonderful people, chatting, playing and sharing really weird stuff.\
Then there’s the iOS app that just this week has been submitted to the Apple store (Coming soon™) and not that long ago the [Android App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.socialsavanna) got a huge overhaul!

I’m sat here rebuilding the website ready for our first real businessesy outing and I can’t help but think how amazing all our members are, we talk almost every day more than I talk to my family! some of you I have on Facebook, twitter and there isn’t a day that goes by where you don’t make me laugh.

Even last night, an exchange I had with a member and her mum on Facebook and then other members joined in. I was crying with laughter.

 

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/7Nq18Ax1-150x150.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/7Nq18Ax1.png)

I know i’m not American (unless I am and someone didn’t tell me) but thank you.

It doesn’t matter if you’ve been on the site just a few days or since we started, whether we’ve met or not. You’re all absolutely amazing and I wanted to thank you.

Whatever you’re doing today, don’t be alone, come and [chat](https://chatplayshare.com). Both Ena and I will be there for most of the evening and I’m sure countless other members will be 🙂
