**Halloween Competition**

[![Halloween Competition](/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/halloweenpic.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/halloweenpic.jpg)

This is super simple! All you need to do is:

\*          Make your scariest halloween picture in MSPaint (no Photoshop – we’ll know by the pixels!)

\*          Submit your picture to the gallery on Imgur with the tag, “Spooky Savanna” (or you can include it in the picture)

\*          Send a link to the post via PM to **[TheBritishAreComing](http://imgur.com/user/TheBritishAreComing)** or by email to [**hello@socialsavanna.com**](hello@socialsavanna.com).

The three posts with the most upvotes (doesn’t have to leave “user sub”) before 23:59  Friday 31st October EDT, will win a year’s free premium subscription to Social Savanna.

 

**Your submission must be OC and must be your own work.**

My submission (and instructions) are [**here**](http://imgur.com/gallery/TgpA0).

 

**Movember**

[![Movember](/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/movember.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/movember.jpg)

Movember is coming up at the end of this week.

We want to support the movement by highlighting profiles and providing a place where you guys can put your Movember donation link.

If you have any suggestions, let us know **[here](https://socialsavanna.com/democracy/movember)**. We hope you’ll join us in this worthy and fun cause. 🙂

 

**Secret Santa**

[![Secret Santa](/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/secretsanta.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/secretsanta.jpg)

Last, but not least, we’re looking at ways of doing a Secret Santa for all those interested (in a way that’s safe). If you’re interested in joining in, just send an email with your Imgur username to [**santa@socialsavanna.com**](santa@socialsavanna.com).

It’s working properly now, so if you want to unsubscribe from newsletters, just click **[here](https://socialsavanna.com/newsletters/unsubscribe/1336197318cfe8b7c027417eec7ecdbc)**.
