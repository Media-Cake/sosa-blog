Wow. Just wow, we’ve made it another year, and what a year that was! 

As a business we’ve seen a lot of changes over the last year, we started the with a small army of developers and mods but ended the year with just Umbrena and I, No more Tinychat and all but 3 chat rooms have been deleted.

But as they say, as an era ends a new day begins and in that same year, we’ve become [SoSa](https://chatplayshare.com) with a brand new domain (https\://chatplayshare.com) we’ve now got both an [Android App](http://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.socialsavanna) and [iPhone App,](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/sosa/id1316006181?ls=1\&mt=8) we have weekly game nights and two real world meetups happening this year ([Hyde Park](https://chatplayshare.com/meetups/sosa-does-hyde-park) and [NYC](https://chatplayshare.com/meetups/sosa-does-new-york)).

Very shortly you will see a whole new [SoSa](https://chatplayshare.com), it will have familiar groundings but with a modernised interface and great new features that you’ve all been asking for as well as lots of bugs fixed. Then we will start our journey into advertising and growing [SoSa](https://chatplayshare.com) for the first time.

I can’t express how excited I am to see how much we grow and change as a platform, and community over the next 12 months. It fills me with so much joy to see so many people from all walks of life coming together and making new friendships and having a good time on SoSa.

It’s truly becoming a place where people all over the world can meet, chat, play, and share. 

For everyone that has been part of SoSa during the last 4 years, thank you so much for being part of the journey and I hope you’ll join us for the next 4 years.

Lots of love

James and Ena
