Hello again dear SoSarians, SoSaers, SoSapeoples?

This week we are featuring the long play video of our Trouble in Terrorist Town video. This footage is from the first time we played this game mode on Garry’s Mod.

[Click here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-bEf-dhvZ4) to watch the video!

For those of you who have never played before, everyone is a terrorist and approximately 25% of the terrorists are selected to be traitors. As a traitor, the goal is to work together (when there are more than one) with your fellow traitors and slowly kill off the detective and innocents. The detective is armed with certain tools to help the innocents discover who the traitors are.

Here you can see an example of a Traitor looking at a fellow Traitor. Notice the special indicator in the bottom left corner and the T above your comrades head.

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/traitor-300x169.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/traitor.png)

The game also has a points system, so you will lose points if you mindlessly kill other innocents. This is what makes the game fun as we try to rationalize (hah!) our kills. As always, we are not allowed to blurt out who the terrorists are once we die, in order to keep the game fair and mysterious. We got to play a couple different maps, including a cottage, a boat, a childrens room (wait until you see it, it is bright pink) and a rooftop.

Below we have an example of what the winning screen looks like – just the simple irony of “innocent” terrorists winning is great.

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Traitor2-300x169.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Traitor2.png)
