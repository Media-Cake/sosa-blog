Hi SoSa friends and welcome to another gaming video! This week we are showing you the footage of our OpenRA video and specifically we are playing Red Alert.  This is the first part of the video, stay tuned for the second part coming in the following weeks.

Click [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=43liXwEiOuk) to watch the video.

OpenRA Red Alert is a real-time strategy game, where you construct buildings and defenses and build up infantry units, aircraft and navy vessels in order to beat out your enemies and be the all powerful conqueror of them all.

You start off with a small area (pictured below) and the goal is to expand while growing your resources quicker than your enemies.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/openra1-300x169.png)

Once you get some basic things going, you start expanding a lot quicker and that’s where the game gets really fun and will vary with strategies (or lack there-of). Check out how much the map expands once you get some units going.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/openra2-300x169.png)

Listen to the members reason as whether or not they should team up together to take the Artificial Intelligence out and if they should form alliances. Watch them develop questionable tactics and strategies to overcome their opponents.
