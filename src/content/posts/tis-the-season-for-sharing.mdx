It’s Christmas – well, maybe a few days after – and at this time of year, we like to show our appreciation to our favourite people: you.

So, in the spirit of sharing, we made you a thing! A thing to share other things! A sharey thing to share your things with other things that people share!

**You can now upload your images, videos, and audio files directly to Social Savanna! Yay!**\
\
That means you can post your own pictures, music, or anything you like up to 10mb, straight into chat or comments with the simple click of a button.

Imagine the gifs, recordings and videos of you eating sausage rolls that you can show people!\
The possibilities are endless!

“But how?” you ask. It’s simple! Just use the shiny new button!

[![screenshot-socialsavanna.com 2015-12-30 13-30-59](/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/screenshot-socialsavanna.com-2015-12-30-13-30-59.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/screenshot-socialsavanna.com-2015-12-30-13-30-59.png)

But wait, there’s more! We’ve got even more things coming:

- When we’ve worked out security risks, you will be able to share anything you like – even those sausage roll recipes!
- With our new privacy settings, you get to decide exactly who gets to see the content you deliver to the community, giving you more control when sharing.

Many doughnuts were eaten and many keys tapped to bring this feature to your screens. We hope you enjoy using it and that it improves your experience. Go and use it! And don’t forget to tell us what you think!
