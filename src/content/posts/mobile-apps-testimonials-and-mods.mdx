Firstly, welcome new Imgurians! We’ve had about 6,000 join in the last two weeks. I hope you’re enjoying the site!

These last few weeks we have mostly been doing some tweaks and fixes across Social Savanna.

 

**Testimonials**

August 27th marks six months of Social Savanna and we’d love to make a post on Imgur to show how much good has come from the site.

I know plenty of friendships and relationships have formed because of the site, but also it’s helped some people through hard times, so we’d love to hear your story.

Please email **[hello@socialsavanna.com](mailto:hello@socialsavanna.com)** with your story and include any relevant pictures. 🙂

 

[![Android app!](/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/googleplaystore.png)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.socialsavanna)

 

**Android Mobile App**

We have finally finished our mobile chat app! It’s been a long time in development and I hope you all enjoy it (please, please rate it and let us know if you have any problems so we can fix).

 

**Free** ad-powered version **(for a subscriber)**:

[**https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.socialsavanna**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.socialsavanna)

 

**Ad-Free** please go to **(not for subscribers)**:

[**https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.socialsavanna.paid**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.socialsavanna.paid)

 

Over the coming weeks, we will update this app adding more of the site’s features to it.

 

**Kitty Cam**

Many of you will notice the addition of Kitty Cam. This is available when using the site from a desktop computer 🙂 We regularly get sleepy kitty piles.

![Kitty Cam](/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/kittycam.jpg)

 

**Meetup Notifications**

Starting this week, you will start receiving emails for upcoming meetups in your local area. Please use the notifications tab in the settings area to stop or adjust these.

![Meetup notifications](/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/meetups-close.jpg)

 

**New Chat Commands**

After lots of requests, we’ve added a few small commands to chat. /whisper /me and /slap

![Commands](/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/commands.jpg)
