I pitched! it was scary and fun at the same time and I was part of a [World Record](https://www.vmbvoom.com/news/record-breaking-quantities-of-voom).

Unfortunately it wasn’t enough and we didn’t get through to the semi-finals,  we’re still waiting for for feedback and as soon as I have the video I’ll post it for you to see but in the interim we’ve been approached by 2 potential investors 🙂

We’re busy getting the business plan ready and in the next few weeks we will be discussing further with several potential investors to help us make the dream a reality.

## What does investment mean?

Getting investment would mean we can complete the platform, finalize the iPhone app, start doing regular meet-ups all over the world, introduce our own voice and video chat and create a global platform where people can join and make new friends and maybe even more.

Investment would allow us to grow, we sit at just over 81,000 members and with investment we would be at 1 million with in a year. potentially 10 million in the next 5 years.

## What are we you working on at the moment?

We’re working on a few things, the iPhone app is being developed slowly. We’re working on Social games which I hope will be live shortly and focusing on the overall newbie experience so we can start advertising ourselves before we get investment.

## Can I help?

Yes, absolutely. The biggest thing you can do for us right now is jump in [chat](https://socialsavanna.com/messaging/public), [discussions ](https://socialsavanna.com/discuss)and on the [gaming servers](https://socialsavanna.com/games). Start conversations, discussions and play. This means we can focus on getting the site and platform ready and help us grow 🙂
