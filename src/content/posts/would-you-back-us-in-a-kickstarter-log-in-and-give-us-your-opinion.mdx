Hello all,

We are looking at ways of raising capital to push SoSa to the next level.

We have been offered investment, but would like to avoid this route unless we get the right investor who understands the community.

Among other things, we would like to do:

\*     More sponsored meetups across the globe

\*     Finish the group and private video chat

\*     Update the chat

\*     Build a social gaming platform

\*     Create a more integrated experience with other social and gaming networks (Steam, Xbox, PS Network, etc)

\*     Improve the layout and flow of the site

We need to raise about $40,000, and pledges would start at $1.

Would you contribute to a Kickstarter if we did one?

It’d be great to hear your thoughts!

 

James
