We’ve opened up.

This last year, we’ve had issues with Imgur’s API. Last week, most people we’re able to log in.

Based on this and your feedback, we have opened the site up to everyone.

After logging in, go to **[https://socialsavanna.com/settings/#account](https://socialsavanna.com/settings/#account)**, and you can now link your account to the following:

\*     Facebook

\*     Reddit

\*     Google+

\*     Twitter

\*     Imgur

If we can work with Imgur in the future, we would love to.

For the moment, we want to keep making the community better for all of you with meetups, better features, and improving the website and app.

 

**We Need Mods!**

If you want to be part of the awesome mod team, then email **[hello@socialsavanna.com](mailto:hello@socialsavanna.com)** and tell us a bit about yourself.

We want people who are all about the community (not the ban hammer!).

 

**Kickstarter!**

We are seeking investors or contemplating a Kickstarter. Please vote: **[https://socialsavanna.com/democracy/kickstarter](https://socialsavanna.com/democracy/kickstarter)**

 

**Reddit**
