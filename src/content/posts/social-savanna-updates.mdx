**Dedicated Gaming and Teamspeak**

![Dedicated Gaming and Teamspeak](/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dedicatedgamingandteamspeak-1024x548.png)

_Minecraft_ is the first game to go up (Feed the Beast Direwolf 20 1.7) and we need people to help test. So, if you’d like to be a part of this, send us an email (**hello\@socialsavanna.com**) with your Imgur username and _Minecraft_ username.

Until the end of January 2015, all of this will be free as we make sure it’s a premium service.

After that, it will cost $1-$1.99 a month for acces to all the game servers and teamspeak.

**Find Local Imgurians**

![Find Local Imgurians](/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/findlocalimgurians-1024x757.jpg)

You can now search for Imgurians who have a photo and your search choices are remembered when you leave the page.

Both of these features were suggested and voted for by you. 🙂

 

**Country Flags**

![Country Flags](/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/countryflags.jpg)

 

 

Such a simple change, but it does add a bit of colour to the chat room and makes it easier to identify other Imgurians in the same country as you.

This was a user suggestion that people voted on.

No idea what all this is about?

Well, back in February, we made a social network just for Imgurians to meet, chat and play with each other.

We have already had our fair share of relationships, friendships and all the good stuff since then. We are always adding new stuff, making it better.

Anyway, if you haven’t already checked it out, then you should.

It’s free, it has chat, Q\&A, meetups, messaging, kittycam. We’ll soon have match-making and we’re constantly working to make it better.
