Hi guys, and welcome to another week and another gaming blog. This week we are gracing you with the long play video of our Teeworlds meetup.

Click [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HuoYHJJpdb4) to watch the video.

What is Teeworlds you ask? Teeworlds is an adorable little fast paced shooter arcade game where tee balls try to murder each other with guns, cannons, and hammers. Enjoy the footage of everyone playing, except TheSire, who spent most of the match grapple hooking members and trying to throw them down a hole.

First we played on this green map that did not have any holes. Have an action shot of HodorLebowski shooting TheBritishAreComing as CannibalFox tries to hammer him. (hah)

 ![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/teeworlds2-300x169.png)

We then switched over to a map that had a giant hole in the ground, so you had to be extra careful. Well, some of us took this to our advantage. Here we have a live action shot of TheSire committing suicide as a tee, while grapple hooking CannibalFox to her death as well. What a sly bastard.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/teeworlds-300x169.png)
