After a few weeks of getting you glorious people to [vote for us](/we-need-your-vote/), I got the opportunity as part of a pitchathon competition run by [Virgin Media Business](https://www.vmbvoom.com) to pitch to:

- **Jamal Edwards** (SB.TV),
- **Andy Fishburn** (Virgin Investment),
- **Julie Deane** (The Satchel Company),
- **Peter Kelly** (MD Virgin Media Business)
- and **Danielle Restivo** (LinkedIn)

I’ve never pitched to people with so much influence before so I spent my days and nights practising, revising, practising some more. In the shower, as I made dinner, even as I walked home through the streets after a night out (I got a few crazy looks for that).

We were given 3 minutes to pitch and then 5 minutes to answer questions, everyone was friendly and welcoming.\
From the pitcher point of view – we were all in the same boat. A lot of nerves, some alcohol and plenty of VOOM!

Unfortunately we didn’t make it through to the [semi-final](/whats-next-for-social-savanna/) but I enjoyed the experience and learnt a lot from it, giving me the confidence to start pushing forward for investment with [Social Savanna](https://socialsavanna.com) and look to the future with my dream.

The video below is the end result 🙂 I think I did well even if nerves did get the better of me and you’ll definitely be seeing more from me in the future 🙂

Let me know what you think!
