It’s 2017 and that means we need a Member of the Month for January! We’re finally back on track with things so these will be happening every month!

To start this year off, we’ve chosen to dedicate our Member of the Month award to…

[Orangebolt222](https://socialsavanna.com/profile/Orangebolt222), aka Jared!

Jared is an active, kind member of the community and we love him!

![Look at him! look how awesome he is!](/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/jared-dragon-300x94.jpg)

Look at him! look how awesome he is!

 

As game nights become more frequent and more varied, with various members running their own games as well as our SoSa run nights, we like to take a step back and appreciate those who take time out of their day to provide others with a chance to game with each other. Jared is a part of this! He runs Dungeons and Dragons nights (which are pretty fun, so definitely keep an eye out for the next session).

Image modified from: [http://dnd.wizards.com/products/tabletop-games/rpg-products/dungeon-masters-screen](http://dnd.wizards.com/products/tabletop-games/rpg-products/dungeon-masters-screen) if you love D\&D stuff go buy things!

Jared often greets and welcomes members pleasantly and helps to create the atmosphere that we like – warm and welcoming – to everybody. It makes a huge difference for a newbie to join and be greeted. Keep doing this!

Jared’s interests include general tech and gaming, but one of his biggest passions lies in music. While he enjoys being a listener, listening to various punk bands like Rancid, Ramshackle Glory, and Days n Daze, he also an avid player! Instruments Jared plays include guitar, piano, violin, viola, and cello! As he puts it, he’s a “strings kinda guy”. Not mention being a classically trained double bassist.

And finally, how could one mention Jared without mentioning RJ? RJ is his companion in life. He wears bow ties, eats eggs, doesn’t bork or yip, and is featured on our facebook page perhaps more often than he should be. We absolutely love RJ, and you should love him too!
