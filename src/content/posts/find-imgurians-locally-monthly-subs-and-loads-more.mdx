Hey all,

It’s been a busy week this week, with a lot of new changes, so here goes!

[![Monthly subscriptions!](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/star-150x150.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/star.png)

 

**Monthly Subscriptions now available**

You don’t need a subscription, but getting one gives you more access across the site, and helps us continue building Social Savanna.

**If you want to help, but can’t afford a subscription, see the bottom of this post for ways you can help us.&#xA0;**&#x1F642;

 

 

[![Messaging and Comments](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/cloudchat-150x150.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/cloudchat.png)

 

**Messaging and Comments**

Private messaging should be a lot quicker and problems like duplicate messages have been fixed.

Both private messaging and comments across the site now support links and images, so you can share Imgur pictures and links with other Imgurians.

 

 

[![Searching](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/mappic-150x150.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/mappic.png)

 

 

**Meetups and Profiles distance** **searching&#xA0;**

We have added “search by distance” to Meetups and profiles so you can easily find other Imgurians close to you, and Meetups happening in your area.

 

 

 

[![Blocking and Privacy](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/okay.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/okay.png)

 

**Blocking and Privacy**

Privacy options have been simplified and you can block Imgurians who you don’t want contact with (although, we recommend reporting anyone who is harassing you!).

 

 

 

[![Profiles](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/snapshot-150x150.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/snapshot.png)

 

**Profiles**

We’ve added Q & A to profiles (this will feed our matching algorithm shortly).

Finally, other users can comment on your profile, and we have added relationship status, education and work. 🙂

 

 

 

We’ve got a load more coming this week – most notably a development blog, live site feed, and instant messaging (similar to Facebook) the site over.

Any questions, please email hello\@socialsavanna.com, and we hope you enjoy all the new changes. 🙂

[**https://socialsavanna.com**](https://socialsavanna.com)

 

PS If you want to help us, but can’t afford a subscription, any of the following helps…

\*     Turning off AdBlock for our site (if you see any noisy auto-play ads, let us know so we can turn these off)

\*     Link to your Social Savanna profile from your Imgur profile

\*     Screenshot what you see on the site and what you enjoy, and share with the people of Imgur, and get other Imgurians to join us. 🙂
