Hey! you probably didn’t think it’d been a month since I last updated did you?

It’s been a month since my last update and 6 weeks since my last Alpha release!

Before I share what happened, I want to say thank you to everyone for their kind messages of support over the last month, as well as the Patreons continuing to support SoSa.

It’s really helped centre me and keep me motivated to working with SoSa!

## So what happened?

Being completely transparent, a lot has been going on at work. We’re building something incredible that will change an entire sector – but the team is still growing and with that comes pains.

As their tech lead, I wanted to make sure that we kept things moving even when we were struggling and that meant I was working long days and weekends but at the end of it – i needed some time to recoup emotionally and mentally.

On top of that, not that long ago, I started some life changing medication which has been wreaking havoc with my emotional state.

So I kept my head down as much as possible and focused on doing tiny bits of work to move us forward.

Cool Story Bro, but what now?

## Framework Rebuild / Code freshen up

Framework rebuild is finally finished, I went in to this in more detail in my [last update](/sprint-20-lets-get-nerdy/) but during this sprint i’ve also had to refactor the app and the chat bots to support the new client methodology.

On top of that, i’ve fixed some bugs inside the app that were related to connectivity, I’ve moved the client library to be at the top layer of the app (so it can always be connected and accessible no matter where in the app you are) AND i’ve refactored some parts of the app, cleaning up the code generally as I introduced.

Chat server now supports more than just chat, becoming our general purpose server.

<figure className="aligncenter size-large is-resized"><img src="/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/image.png" alt="Stats stats the wonderful fruit the more you eat? the more you toot?!?!?!" width="485" height="504" /></figure>

Our Domain authority is up to 27 from 23! Seems Moz found about 100 domains linking to our website that weren’t there before. 

A bit unusual so I’m going to keep an eye on that – quick growths can usually be a sign of someone buying links and as I’m not buying links that can suggest an attack and i need to mass disvow to stop google deranking us.

[![Webmaster tools doesn't really give me meaningful information yet here we are](/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/image-1.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/image-1.png)

Our average search position has improved from 24.2 to 23.2, our impressions are up from 1.78k to 3.19k and our click through rate has gone down again from 2.4% to 2.2% but more overall clicks!
