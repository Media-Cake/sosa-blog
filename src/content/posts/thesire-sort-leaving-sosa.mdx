Hi all, I have some news. Some terribly sad news. News that I am confident will bring tears to the masses… 

As of January 2018 I will no longer be working at SoSa. It’s been a wicked 2 years and I have enjoyed getting to know the community, meeting/playing with you lot, and the amount of laughs and just great times we’ve shared. I may talk about what’s next for me at a later date. 🙂

That being said, I won’t be leaving SoSa! I will still be around, I might even be a mod (that’s up to James), so I’ll still be annoying you all on a fairly regular basis. You all have to carry on worshiping me, obviously, so don’t feel like this is going to change much.

I want to be clear that this decision comes without animosity, I’m not sacked/fired, and James and I will continue to be passionate lovers for years to come.

Thanks for reading, and please feel free to send me as many cards and gifts as you’d like.
