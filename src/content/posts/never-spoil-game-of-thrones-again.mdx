With the last AMAZING episode of Game of Thrones behind us, it occurred to me.

So many of our members want to talk about Game of Thrones but are cautious about spoiling it for other people. Well fear no more!

In record time I’ve added a [Spoiler chat room](https://socialsavanna.com/messaging/public/spoilerchat) where you can have live conversations with other Game Of Throne fans about the latest episode without the worry of other people having it ruined for them.

![spoilerchat](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/spoilerchat.jpeg)

 

## But wait. There’s more!

We’ve also made it so you can blur out text in [discussions](https://socialsavanna.com/discuss), all you need to do is surround your text with $$ and it’ll become a spoiler

![text](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/text.jpg)

Then when you hit the send button, the text will be censored. All you have to do is simply click the text and it’ll show the spoiler.

![Before](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/before.jpg)

Before

![After](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/after.jpg)

After

The same works in the [discussion ](https://socialsavanna.com/discuss/Movies/spoiler-test)itself and in chat (although keep all spoilers in chat to the “spoilers” chat).

To make the site a bit safer from spoilers, any discussions that may contain spoilers will show a little warning sign.

![list](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/list.jpeg)

I hope you all enjoy this new feature, and as always suggestions are welcome and appreciated 🙂

James
