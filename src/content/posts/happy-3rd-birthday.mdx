Just a few days ago we hit our 3 year anniversary. For the last two, I’ve talked about how Social Savanna has affected me and my life and how the community has changed me, whilst that never stops being true, Social Savanna is about the community. It’s more than me. It’s about you.

It’s amazing that over the space of 3 years you’ve talked non-stop about everything. From the really serious issues, such as whether a duck could fight a horse, to the quite frankly ridiculous: The Trump administration.

You have spent 3 years of meeting each other, from meeting down the pub or playing at one of our gaming meets to crossing continents for road trips and even finding the love.

With that said, I think it’s safe to say the 1 week of TheSire’s puns however was 1 week too much!

Here are some highlights from the last year
