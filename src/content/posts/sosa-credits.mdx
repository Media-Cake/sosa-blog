A little while back I spoke to some members about introducing credits on [Social Savanna](https://socialsavanna.com).\
Something that would let you give back to the community and something that would reward you for amazing comments, discussions etc.

Also as a way for those who can’t afford a subscription to still be able to support the site and get extra goodies!\
Well, today I would like to introduce, SoSa Credits!

## What the heck are SoSa Credits?

It’s a virtual currency that you can exchange subscriptions, gift other members and use to reward people for being awesome.

## Gimme

You want some huh? Just login and to [https://socialsavanna.com/credits](https://socialsavanna.com/credits).\
You can earn credits by taking surveys, installing apps, watching videos etc as well as being active on the site either in chat or discussions.

![screenshot-socialsavanna.com 2016-03-07 09-53-55](/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/screenshot-socialsavanna.com-2016-03-07-09-53-55-1024x607.png)

## What’s in it for me?

At the moment, you can exchange the credits for subscriptions (25 credits = 1 day of Pro) but we’re working with a few companies which will allow you to convert your credits into real world goodies, vouchers, subscription boxes etc.

![screenshot-socialsavanna.com 2016-03-07 09-54-54](/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/screenshot-socialsavanna.com-2016-03-07-09-54-54-1024x882.png)

## Okay, great but what about Social Savanna?

We get paid by the advertiser / company when you fill out an offer, take a survey, install an app etc to help support development, hosting and management of the site and [app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.socialsavanna).

Aside from money. If you chat, create discussions or comment on discussions this helps the community grow and makes the site far more useful to everyone 🙂

## Guilding

I’ve always loved on reddit that if you saw a though provoking or quality comment, you could reward that person with something extra. Something more like a thumbs up.\
So we’ve done the same on Social Savanna! If you have credits then you can guild a comment and soon we’ll expand it to discussions and even chat.

![screenshot-socialsavanna.com 2016-03-07 09-57-58](/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/screenshot-socialsavanna.com-2016-03-07-09-57-58-1024x184.png)

## Community Pledge

But wait, there’s more! You can also contribute directly to the rest of the community!\
When you do a community pledge, everyone who has been active on the site in the last 5 minutes will get a share of the credits.

EG: If you donate $10 and there are 10 people active on the site then they will all receive 100 credits each.

![screenshot-socialsavanna.com 2016-03-07 09-54-09](/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/screenshot-socialsavanna.com-2016-03-07-09-54-09-1024x225.png)

 

## How do I see many balance?

Either visit the credits screen (accessible via the menu) or just click the menu in the top left 🙂

![screenshot-socialsavanna.com 2016-03-05 23-56-01](/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/screenshot-socialsavanna.com-2016-03-05-23-56-01.png)

 

So what are you waiting for? Go support your glorious community 🙂
