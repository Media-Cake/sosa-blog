Hey everyone,

So, let’s recap (for those who have been living under a rock)! In December, thousands of you asked my team to build a social network for you.

We built it and launched two months ago – you all came and it was glorious.

Well, it’s still glorious. There’s over 30,000 Imgurians on the site, chatting, getting to know each other, meeting up, and even some hook-ups and dates, as well. 🙂

If you’d still like updates and aren’t part of the site, sign up: **[http://mad.ly/signups/97554/join](http://mad.ly/signups/97554/join)**.

When we have upcoming events (like Imgurians movie nights and reading nights), I will still post about them here and on Imjur. 🙂

James (TheBritishAreComing)

 

**Update #1 – Autojoin chatrooms**

[![Autojoin chatrooms](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/autojoinchatrooms.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/autojoinchatrooms.png)

This was a user request. If you’re big on the chatrooms, you can set which room you’d like to join by default by just going into the settings area.

 

**Update #2 – Your profile location**

[![Profile location](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/profilelocation-1024x513.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/profilelocation.png)

Now you can use Google Maps to set your location, making the “Find Imgurian” area much more accurate. Remember: you don’t have to set your exact address, you can just enter the city or state.

 

**Update #3 – Images**

[![Images](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/images.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/images.png)

We’ve added privacy options to images, and you can now also add a description and change the order of how they appear on your profile.

 

**Update #3.1 – Imgur integration**

[![Imgur integration](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/imgurintegration-1024x560.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/imgurintegration.png)

We’ve integrated your Ingur gallery, as well, so you can pull images you’ve uploaded onto Imgur. 🙂

 

**Update #3.2 – Editing your thumbnail**

[![Editing your thumbnail](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/editthumbnail.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/editthumbnail.png)

Loads of you asked to be able to edit your thumbnail, so here it is in all of its glory… TAADDDAAA!

 

**Update #4**

[![Friends list update](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/friendlist.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/friendlist.png)

Minor update, but now your profile doesn’t have a big long list of friends – you can click to see more.

 

**Update #5 – Q & A section**

[![Q & A section](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/qandasection-711x1024.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/qandasection.png)

Quite a major revamp with the Q & A section – now all of the categories show and you can browse them individually.

Also, comments have been ordered by points, as requested. 🙂

 

**Update #6 – Meetup photos**

[![Meetup photos](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/meetupphotos-1024x850.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/meetupphotos.png)

Something else you guys have asked for is the ability to add photos to a Meetup. This week we will also be integrating this so you can post to Imgur.
