Hello SoSa friends and welcome to another gaming video!

This week we are showing you the highlight footage from our Golf with Your Friends meetup.  You may have seen some other short clips of this video around our Facebook site and even the full video on our blog, but we can not resist giving you more and the voice clips alone are just great.

Click [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XUz8XG12v2E) to watch the video!

For this highlights video we played the Forest map, as ordinary balls and the Oasis map, as crazy shapes.

First, we have this screenshot of the first Golf with Your Friends map, where we practiced our skills as normal balls. Check out our scoreboard from the first game!

![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/scores-300x169.png)

Second, we have this lovely screenshot from the Oasis map, where we struggled to fit various shapes with bouncy ground and low gravity in the hole. In this screenshot in particular, check out the amount of strokes a frustrated FrogginBullfish has to get his Christmas ornament/bauble into the hole.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/trollinstruggle-300x169.png)

If you are not sold on that, just tune in to hear our lovely voices, laughter and screaming. Or tune in to see our colourful balls, whatever floats your boat.
